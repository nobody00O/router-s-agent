Know if your router is being watched.

Register your router's network specs, run a small local scan on your own network, and get plain-English alerts if an unrecognized device joins or if something on your network is sending data to a known tracker/telemetry destination.

How it works

  1. Sign up and register your router's subnet, interface, and known devices.
  2. Download the small local scanning agent and run it on your own network.
  3. The agent reports results here — your dashboard shows devices and alerts.

Note: this site cannot scan your network by itself — no website can see inside your home network from the outside. The local agent does the actual scanning, on your machine, with your permission.
